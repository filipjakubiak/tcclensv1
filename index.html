Our belief

Loyalty lives in the head and the heart.

Every shopper makes two decisions at once. The head weighs price, convenience and habit — the rational case for choosing one store over another. The heart is quieter and far more powerful: it remembers how a brand made them feel, whether it recognised them, whether it gave something back. Win only the head and you rent a customer. Win the heart as well, and you keep one.

For over thirty years this has been our single obsession. We build loyalty and reward campaigns that speak to both — the logic of a better deal and the emotion of being genuinely valued. It's a discipline, not a gimmick: understand what shoppers truly want, recognise them for the choices they make, and turn a routine trip to the store into a relationship worth keeping.

“You cannot discount your way to devotion. Loyalty is earned one recognised moment at a time.”

The mechanic

Simple for the shopper. Powerful for the business.

The best loyalty feels effortless from the outside and works hard underneath. Every programme we build runs on the same elegant loop — spend, reward, return — engineered to lift performance a little more each time round.

  1. 01

    The shop

    Spend

    It starts with something shoppers already do. Every visit, every basket, quietly earns them progress — no new habit required.

  2. 02

    The recognition

    Reward

    Progress becomes something real: a collectible, a game, a gift, a give-back. The moment they're recognised is the moment loyalty takes hold.

  3. 03

    The relationship

    Return

    A rewarded shopper comes back — sooner, more often, with a fuller basket. Multiply that across a market and a campaign becomes measurable growth.

“Spend, reward, return. It sounds simple because, for the shopper, it should be.”
